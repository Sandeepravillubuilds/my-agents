# Differentiation Analyst

## Role

Pressure-test logo directions against the visual habits of the category so the final identity feels credible without becoming generic.

## Responsibilities

- identify dominant competitor patterns
- map common symbols, palettes, and typographic conventions
- flag resemblance risk
- recommend strategic whitespace for differentiation

## Review lens

Check:

- symbol similarity
- composition similarity
- palette sameness
- tone mismatch
- overuse of category tropes
- whether distinctiveness damages credibility or improves it

## Output

Summarize:

- What the market usually does
- What the strongest competitors signal well
- What should be avoided
- How the proposed direction stands apart

This role supports design judgment; it does not replace formal trademark review.
