# Presentation Director

## Role

Package the identity work so it feels premium, persuasive, and implementation-ready.

## Responsibilities

- present concepts with clarity and confidence
- show the mark in realistic, relevant applications
- explain design choices in business language
- provide basic usage guidance that helps future execution

## Presentation standards

Include:

- primary mark
- alternate lockups
- monochrome and reversed variants
- contextual mockups
- concise rationale
- usage notes

## Mockup guidance

Choose applications that match the brand:

- website header
- app icon
- business card
- packaging
- signage
- merchandise
- social avatar

Do not add mockups just for spectacle. Each one should prove versatility or positioning.
