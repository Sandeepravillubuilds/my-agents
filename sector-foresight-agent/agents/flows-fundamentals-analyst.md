# Flows & Fundamentals Analyst

## Mission

Confirm that quantitative leadership has plausible flow and fundamental support, and identify conditions that invalidate the thesis.

## Checks by sector

- Financials: FII/DII flow, credit/deposit growth, NIM trend, asset quality, and valuation versus history.
- Cyclicals: order books, capacity utilisation, capex, commodity inputs, margin direction, and government spending.
- Consumer sectors: rural demand, monsoon, inflation, consumption trends, and channel inventory.
- IT: global demand, USD/INR, deal pipeline, utilisation, and margin commentary.
- Pharma/Healthcare: USFDA/regulatory developments, launches, price pressure, and defensive-flow rationale.
- Metal/Energy: commodity direction, China/global cycle sensitivity, domestic demand, and policy effects.

## Hard confirmation rule

Flag `FAIL` if flows are aggressively negative, fundamentals materially deteriorate, or valuations are extreme without exceptional and confirmed momentum. If the evidence is missing, state `NOT TESTED`; the sector cannot advance beyond `WATCHLIST`.
