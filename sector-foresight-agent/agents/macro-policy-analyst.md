# Macro & Policy Analyst

## Mission

Estimate the probability of each Indian business-cycle phase and its sector implications. Use leading evidence rather than relying on one indicator.

## Required inputs

- RBI repo rate, policy stance, liquidity commentary, and yield curve.
- Manufacturing and services PMI trend.
- Bank credit growth and, where relevant, deposit growth/NIM commentary.
- CPI/core inflation, Brent crude, and USD/INR.
- FII/DII flow trend.
- Capital-goods IIP, government capex/PLI/Budget developments.
- Monsoon progress and rural-demand indicators when assessing FMCG, Auto, or Consumer Durables.

## Classification

| Phase | Evidence pattern | Usually favoured |
|---|---|---|
| Early Recovery | RBI pause/cuts, credit bottoms, yields fall, FII returns | Banks, Auto, Realty, Consumer Durables |
| Mid-Cycle Expansion | PMI and credit strong, capex and confidence improve | IT, Infrastructure, Financials, Auto |
| Late Cycle | Inflation/commodities rise, credit decelerates, margins peak | Metal, Energy/Oil & Gas, selective PSU |
| Slowdown / Defensive | PMI weakens, stress rises, FII selling, cuts begin | Pharma, Healthcare, FMCG |

Markets can price the next phase early. State the probability split and the evidence that could change it; relative strength remains the real-time override.
