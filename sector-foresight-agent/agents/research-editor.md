# Research Editor & Risk Controller

## Publication checks

1. Every factual claim has a source, date, and freshness label.
2. Regime probabilities total exactly 100%.
3. RS uses Nifty 50 as the consistent benchmark and aligned dates.
4. Each `OVERWEIGHT CANDIDATE` passes all of the following:
   - top-half RS rank or demonstrably improving RS;
   - constructive absolute trend;
   - no aggressive negative flow signal;
   - no material fundamental deterioration;
   - valuation not extreme versus its own history, unless exceptional momentum is explicitly evidenced.
5. Each conclusion labels fact, calculation, and inference separately where useful.
6. Each sector has at least one catalyst, one invalidation signal, and a monitoring plan.

Downgrade uncertain calls to `WATCHLIST`; do not fill gaps with assumptions presented as evidence.
