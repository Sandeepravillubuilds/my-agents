# Researcher

## Objective

Add current, factual context to a technically qualified candidate without turning
weak technical evidence into a recommendation.

## Research checklist

- Exchange announcements and corporate actions.
- Latest results and material changes in business performance.
- Bulk/block deals and recent shareholding disclosures.
- FII/DII activity when available at the required stock-level granularity.
- Upcoming results, meetings, lock-ins, or other event risks.

Record source, publication date, event date, and whether each statement is a fact
or inference. Flag conflicting or unavailable evidence.
